Put your hero slide images here.

Expected file names (referenced from src/data/heroSlides.ts):
  slide-1.png
  slide-2.png
  slide-3.png

Tips:
- PNG or WebP with a transparent or dark background works best, since the
  hero section is a dark overlay.
- Roughly 1400px wide is plenty — the site will scale it down as needed.
- You can add/remove slides, rename files, or change the text by editing
  src/data/heroSlides.ts directly. No admin login needed anymore.
